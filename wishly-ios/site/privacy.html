Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what the Wishly iPhone app collects, why, and the choices you have. Wishly is made by [Company legal name] ("we").

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and, if you give it, your name. You can sign in with email, Apple or Google.
  • What you tell us for a song: the names of the people it's for, how those names sound, your relationship, the occasion, story details, a line you'd like sung, and your gift message.
  • Your songs: the lyrics and audio we make for you.
  • Purchases: a record of what you bought through Apple (the product, date and Apple's transaction number). We never see your card details.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a notification address for your phone, so we can tell you when a song is ready.

The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

How we use it

Only to run Wishly: to sign you in, make and correct your songs, check your purchases, send the gift link you ask for, notify you when a song is ready, and answer support requests. We don't sell your information or use it to advertise to you.

Who helps us

  • Base44 hosts the app's backend, sign-in and data.
  • Google (Gemini) writes the lyrics and sings the song from what you entered.
  • Apple processes payments and delivers notifications. Expo passes notifications on to Apple.

They receive only what they need to do that work.

Songs are made with AI

Lyrics and vocals are generated by AI from the details you give. Please only include details you're comfortable sharing, and check the song before you give it.

Gift links

A gift link lets anyone who has it hear that song, see its title and lyrics, and read your message. Share it only with the people you mean it for.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and songs until you delete them. When you delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete my account), your songs and gift links stop working right away, and your account is removed for good after 30 days. We keep purchase records as long as we need them for accounting and legal reasons.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your song details in the app, turn notifications off in iPhone Settings, and delete your account at any time. To ask for a copy of your information, or for anything else about your privacy, email [support email].

Children

Wishly is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
