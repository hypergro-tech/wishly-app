Support

Last updated 16 November 2026

We're here to help with your songs, purchases and account.

Email us: hello@trywishly.co
We reply within 2 business days. Please include the email you sign in with.

A name or detail in my song is wrong

Every song includes one free correction. Open the song in the app, tap Fix something, tell us what to change, and we'll make a new version. You can also type how a name sounds (for example "Keeva" for Caoimhe) so it's sung the right way.

How long does a song take?

Usually a few minutes. You can leave the app; the song keeps being made and appears in My songs when it's ready.

How do I give the song?

Once you're happy with the song and approve it, open it in My songs and send the private link by Messages, WhatsApp, email or any app. The person opens it in their web browser, with no app or account needed.

Payments, plans and refunds

  • All purchases are made through Apple. One song is $29. The Monthly ($39) and Yearly ($199) plans give 3 songs every month.
  • Cancel a plan: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Wishly.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • New phone? Sign in, then tap Restore purchases in Profile.

Delete my account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. This deletes your account and your songs, and their gift links stop working. See the Privacy Policy for details.
